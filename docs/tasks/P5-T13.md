## Assumptions and design choices

- `InventoryDigest` is the full pre-accept inventory digest. The new
  `AcceptedInventoryDigest` is the full post-accept digest; keeping both is
  necessary because accepting a snapshot intentionally appends one history
  record and changes current power/coefficient bindings.
- The full digest uses the existing canonical complete bundle-state codec, so
  it includes identity/location/material, burnup/energy/mass, state version,
  I/Xe bytes, current power/snapshot applicability, every history record, and
  coefficient identity. No second partial history rule is introduced.
- The frozen left-endpoint rule remains `Delta_E = P * Delta_t`, with
  `B = InitialBurnup + CumulativeFissionEnergy / HeavyMetalMass`; no numerical
  authority or tolerance was selected.
- Applicable P1-T08 literature rows are `S1-R07`, `S4-R03`, `S4-R04`, `S5-R05`,
  and `S6-R05`; they remain context/methodology or candidate-design evidence,
  not runtime or golden authority.
- Direct CANDU/DONJON5 production authority, full-core coverage, mobile, and
  Unity evidence remain outside this bounded ReducedModel/Core task.
