# P5-T04 - Explicit-time bundle power integration and derived burnup

## Outcome

Status: COMPLETE

P5-T04 implements the bounded explicit-time burnup slice over the current
immutable `BundleInventory`. An accepted bundle-power projection is bound to
an explicit snapshot identity, snapshot time, core-state version, and opaque
source-state digest. The transition applies the approved left-endpoint rule
`Delta_E = P * Delta_t`, derives current burnup from initial burnup plus
cumulative energy over heavy-metal mass, validates every proposed bundle in
canonical order, and returns an immutable replacement inventory only after all
checks succeed.

The transition fails closed on stale time/version/digest, missing or
location-mismatched power, non-finite/negative values, arithmetic overflow,
non-monotone results, and positive energy increments that cannot produce a
representable cumulative-energy increase. `BundleInventory` now rejects an
overflowed derived burnup at construction time. The full P2-T03 power-history
and digest envelope, coefficient-table lookup, and lifecycle-owner commit
remain separate bounded work.

Effectiveness: SUCCESS

## Assumptions and design choices

- The approved P2-T03 equation and units are the authority: `Delta_E = P *
  Delta_t` uses SI watts and seconds to produce joules, and `B = InitialBurnup
  + CumulativeFissionEnergy / m_HM` uses SI J/kg_HM and kg_HM. No literature
  value, tolerance, normalization, coefficient, or golden value was selected.
- The current `BundleInventory` does not own a complete canonical state digest
  or lifecycle object. The accepted power projection therefore requires an
  opaque `Digest32` supplied by the owning state boundary, and the transition
  requires the matching current digest; digest calculation and full binding
  remain outside this bounded contract.
- A power sample represents already accepted node power from the solver. This
  task does not invent a normalization, derive power from flux, or synthesize
  zero power. Zero power is valid and contributes zero interval energy.
- Bundle samples and interval records are canonicalized/evaluated in ascending
  `(ChannelId, BundlePosition, BundleId)` order. The source inventory is never
  mutated; all bundle copies and the replacement inventory are built only
  after validation of the complete proposal.
- The transition reports the proposed single core-state-version increment and
  invalidates the consumed snapshot in its immutable result. The owner that
  holds the simulation clock/lifecycle must commit that proposal atomically;
  this task does not duplicate that owner or implement event-time scheduling.
- The interval must already be split at refuelling/event boundaries by its
  caller. Coefficient-table lookup/interpolation and out-of-range behavior are
  deferred to P5-T05; complete power history, I/Xe, transaction/discharge
  envelopes, and G5 histories remain later work.
- P1-T08 applicability is `S1-R07`, `S4-R03`, `S4-R04`, `S5-R05`, and `S6-R05`
  for background/candidate burnup/refuelling context only. Those rows are not
  runtime or golden authority, and their reported values were not copied.
- `G2` remains exactly `FORCED CLOSED / WAIVED`. P5-T04 is eligible under the
  non-critical gate-overlap policy because it consumes frozen burnup and
  inventory contracts and does not depend on the blocked P4-T06 reference
  admission.
