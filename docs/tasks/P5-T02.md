# P5-T02 - Atomic directional shift and persistent bundle identity movement

## Outcome

Status: COMPLETE

P5-T02 adds an engine-neutral immutable transition that applies a validated
P5-T01 position plan to the current `BundleInventory` contract. It requires a
fully occupied target channel, explicit flow/direction binding, ordered fresh
basic bundle states, and monotonic explicit simulation time. The transition
constructs a replacement inventory only after validation succeeds, moves
retained bundle identities through the approved TowardEndA/TowardEndB mapping,
and returns discharged states in ascending old-position order.

Effectiveness: SUCCESS

The boundary is intentionally limited to the fields represented by the current
`BundleState`: stable identity, channel/position, material key, initial burnup,
cumulative fission energy, heavy-metal mass, and insertion time. The later
complete I/Xe/lifecycle envelope, command/event/discharge persistence,
fresh-template resolution, burnup integration, coefficient lookup, and history
digests are not represented by the current public inventory and remain separate
Phase 5 tasks.

## Assumptions and design choices

- The P5-T01 position plan is the sole source of insertion/discharge boundary
  positions. The transition validates the plan against the target topology and
  channel flow rather than inferring direction from array order.
- The target channel must be fully occupied. For `TowardEndB`, inserted states
  occupy the low positions, retained states move upward by `ShiftCount`, and
  the high-position train is discharged. For `TowardEndA`, inserted states
  occupy the high positions, retained states move downward, and the low train
  is discharged.
- Retained states are copied with `BundleState.WithLocation`; stable identity,
  material key, initial burnup, cumulative energy, heavy-metal mass, and
  insertion time are preserved exactly. Other-channel states remain unchanged.
- Inserted states are caller-supplied pre-resolved basic states. They must be
  ordered by their exact physical destination, use fresh identities absent from
  the live inventory, have explicit material/mass/basic values, use the exact
  effective time, and have zero cumulative energy. Full template identity,
  coefficient binding, and I/Xe envelope equality are deferred because those
  fields are not present in the current `BundleState` contract.
- Atomicity is represented by pure replacement: the existing inventory is
  immutable and invalid validation returns no `RefuelShiftResult`. No command,
  event, clock, lifecycle, discharge-record, or history store is mutated.
- P1-T08 literature applicability is `NotApplicable` for authority or numeric
  selection: this task changes no equation, constant, unit, normalization,
  coefficient, tolerance, reference case, golden value, or physics rule.
  Refuelling-related candidate rows `S1-R07`, `S4-R03`, `S4-R04`, `S5-R05`,
  and `S6-R05` were inspected as background/candidate-design evidence only and
  were not used as runtime authority.
- `G2` remains exactly `FORCED CLOSED / WAIVED`. P5-T02 is eligible under the
  gate-overlap rule because it consumes frozen Core contracts and does not
  depend on the blocked P4-T06 DONJON5 admission or create reference evidence.
