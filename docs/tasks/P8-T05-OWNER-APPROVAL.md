regression (`P8-T05`). The policies are regression harness inputs only and
must not become player-facing automation or a safety/control system.

  Phase 8 runtime, applies policy actions through the approved runtime command

  gameplay outcome/score/turn/loss observables to tests.
- Freeze expected synthetic baseline observables in the policy artifact only
  after they are generated by the deterministic runner. They are balance
  regression baselines, not physical, production, reference, or golden
  reactor values.
- Reject malformed, duplicate, unknown, reordered, wrong-type, nonfinite,
  out-of-order, over-horizon, over-capacity, pack-hash-mismatched, or
  unsupported policy data before execution.
