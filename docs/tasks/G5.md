## Initial review findings and correction

- High: the accepted complete-state burnup path could preserve stale
  `PowerWatts`, `PowerSnapshotId`, and `CoefficientBinding` through the energy
  update and did not provide the required atomic complete snapshot/lifecycle
  burnup commit with every next-state lookup, lifecycle time/core advancement,
  invalidation, and rollback evidence.
- Medium: P5-T10 constructed cumulative energy directly from approved
  project-authored burnup values. It is structural mapping and identity/
  accounting evidence, not independent power-to-burnup integration or
  accumulated numerical-drift evidence.

- High: the complete burnup boundary did not authenticate canonical source
  inventory and burnup/energy digests, allowing a reconstructed inventory to
  retain accepted power/history bindings while changing energy or mass.
- High: the public no-table complete-refuelling overload could commit without
  performing and storing post-shift coefficient lookups.

Correction status: `P5-T11` implemented the complete-state burnup transaction,
invalidated legacy energy bindings, added strict post-shift refuelling lookups,
and updated the P5-T10 report/scope wording. `P5-T12` adds canonical source
digest checks and makes the no-table overload fail closed. The third review
then found that only the latest history record was checked at burnup commit;
`P5-T13` adds separate full pre-accept and post-accept inventory digests and a
regression that removes an earlier record while retaining the current record.
The fourth review found that the accepted digest was still caller-controlled;
`P5-T14` stores all three snapshot state digests on the accepted lifecycle and
adds a forged inventory-plus-snapshot regression. The fifth review then found
that the archive omitted the lifecycle-authenticated digest tuple, plus a
High restore-coherence bypass and a Medium restart-test gap. `P5-T15` adds the
schema-v2 archive persistence, and `P5-T16` adds restore-time reconciliation
and post-load snapshot reconstruction. Focused Phase 5 and full T3 checks pass;
the final same-context high-effort G5 review returned `PASS`.
