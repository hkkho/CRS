## Assumptions and design choices

- P2-T03 is Sol-led because it fixes discrete transition semantics, burnup
  integration, units, energy accounting, interpolation, and state lifecycle. A
  Luna implementation worker was not substituted for this specification task.
- Production channels use the P2-T01 12-position `(ChannelId, BundlePosition)`
  node key. V1 binds `EndAtoEndB` to `TowardEndB` and `EndBtoEndA` to
  `TowardEndA`; a command cannot choose the opposite direction.
- `S4` and `S8` are declarative scheme definitions. Each complete scheme is one
  atomic transaction with a train of inserted identities, a train of discharged
  identities, template validation, scratch-state postconditions, and whole-batch
  rollback. Repeated single-bundle commands are not the v1 scheme contract.
- `InitialBurnup` and `CumulativeFissionEnergy` are authoritative bundle
  fields; current `B` is derived as `InitialBurnup + Energy/m_HM`. Mass is
  supplied by a validated fresh-fuel template. Residence time and append-only
  accepted power history remain attached to identities through movement and
  discharge.
- V1 uses explicit left-endpoint integration in `J/kg_HM`, splits intervals at
  refuelling events, invalidates stale snapshots, performs coefficient lookups
  after every burnup/state transition, and requires a fresh P2-T02 solve at the
  exact post-transition state before positive-duration advancement.
- Burnup coefficient tables use strict finite knots, no extrapolation/clamping,
  and canonical linear interpolation. `chi_1` is primary and `chi_2` is
  derived as `1-chi_1`, avoiding an invented tolerance or spectrum repair.
- All numeric examples, UUIDs, masses, powers, burnups, table values, and
  histories are explicitly synthetic placeholders. They are not copied from
  DRAGON5, DONJON5, private listings, or golden data.

### Independent Sol review and review fixes

A fresh empty-context max-reasoning `gpt-5.6-sol` reviewer performed one initial
read-only review and one final disposition after the consolidated fix pass. The
initial review returned `FAIL` with seven findings:

1. High: the candidate represented only one-bundle shifts instead of atomic
   declarative 4-/8-bundle schemes.
2. High: shift direction was not bound to the channel flow enum.
3. High: stale power and coefficient/snapshot state could cross transitions.
4. Medium: equal-time ordering, sequence identity, and whole-batch rollback
   were underdefined.
5. Medium: cumulative energy and authoritative mass accounting were incomplete.
6. Medium: independently interpolated fission-spectrum components could violate
   exact `double` normalization without an approved tolerance.
7. Medium: the hand-worked histories did not cover complete 4-/8-bundle
   schemes, both directions, event splitting, or post-transition snapshots.

The candidate was corrected in one consolidated pass. The same reviewer then
returned final `PASS` with no High/Medium/Low findings and confirmed that all
seven issues were resolved, including S4/S8 movement, endpoint binding, exact
state-version/snapshot lifecycle, canonical UUID/Sequence batch rollback,
energy/mass/residence/power history, canonical chi interpolation, and both
complete synthetic histories. Final reviewed SHA-256 was
`4538d9f1575bf3684933538849fd850b53c89823380386e76a017470905e3f7c`. The
reviewer wrote no files.
