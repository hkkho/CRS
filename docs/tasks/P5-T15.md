## Assumptions and design choices

- The accepted lifecycle remains the authority for the three snapshot digests;
  persistence restores those values as one all-or-none binding rather than
  accepting caller-supplied replacement digests.
- The archive schema version is advanced explicitly because the prior archive
  shape cannot faithfully restore the authenticated lifecycle tuple. Rejecting
  the old shape is fail-closed and avoids silently weakening the contract.
- The full inventory projection is serialized canonically, including retained
  power history and current coefficient/power bindings; this is required for
  the post-restore digest to mean the same state as before save/load.
- The frozen left-endpoint burnup equation and all existing units, signs,
  convergence rules, tolerances, and approved ReducedModel values remain
  unchanged.
- Applicable P1-T08 literature rows are `S1-R07`, `S4-R03`, `S4-R04`,
  `S5-R05`, and `S6-R05`; they remain context/methodology or candidate-design
  evidence, not runtime or golden authority.
- Direct CANDU/DONJON5/DRAGON5 production authority, full-core coverage,
  Unity/device/mobile, and release evidence remain outside this bounded
  ReducedModel/Core task.
