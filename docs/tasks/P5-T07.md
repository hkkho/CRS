# P5-T07 - Phase 5 identity, location, burnup, and energy invariants

## Outcome

Status: COMPLETE

P5-T07 adds an engine-neutral, fail-closed invariant boundary over the
currently represented Phase 5 Core contracts. The validator requires an
explicit expected live bundle count and checks exact count, stable identity
uniqueness, legal unique locations, finite/nonnegative burnup and energy
fields, positive heavy-metal mass, and the exact derived energy-accounting
relation. Burnup interval validation additionally binds canonical records to
accepted power samples and exact binary64 `P * Delta_t` energy, cumulative
energy, derived burnup, time/version state, and monotonicity. Refuelling shift
validation additionally checks flow direction, retained identity/location
preservation, fresh zero-energy insertion, and discharged identity partition.

it does not claim the complete P2-T03 lifecycle, I/Xe, residence, power
history, discharge, or deterministic long-history envelopes.

## Assumptions and design choices

- The approved P2-T01 topology, P2-T03 refuelling/burnup, and P2-T05
  observable/invariant specifications remain authoritative. No equation, unit,
  sign, convergence rule, tolerance, coefficient, or golden value was added
  or changed.
- The expected bundle count is an explicit validator input. This preserves the
  existing ability to represent bounded partial inventories while allowing the
  production caller or a synthetic fixture to require an exact full count.
- Exact binary64 equality is used for energy-accounting and record binding;
  no hidden tolerance is introduced. Canonical iteration order is
  `(ChannelId, BundlePosition, BundleId)`.
- A positive interval energy that does not produce a representable cumulative
  energy increase fails closed, matching the approved P5-T04 transition rule.
- Refuelling validation is limited to the immutable fields currently present
  in `BundleState` and `RefuelShiftResult`. Complete I/Xe, power-history,
  residence, transaction, and discharge-history envelopes remain later work.
- Applicable P1-T08 rows are `S1-R07`, `S4-R03`, `S4-R04`, `S5-R05`, and
  `S6-R05`. They were used only as background/methodology or candidate-design
  context; `S4-R04` is advanced-fuel context outside this runtime slice. No
  literature number, coefficient, tolerance, checksum, or golden value was
  admitted.
- `G2` remains exactly `FORCED CLOSED / WAIVED`. P5-T07 is independent of the
  blocked P4-T06 DONJON5 licensing/data and solver-mapping admission.
