## Assumptions and design choices

- P2-T02 remains authoritative for
  `A1 phi1 = (chi1/k) F`,
  `A2 phi2 = Sigma_s12 phi1 + (chi2/k) F`, and
  `F = nuSigmaF1 phi1 + nuSigmaF2 phi2`; no alternate model was introduced.
- The public linear-solve policy requires the approved deterministic
  `jacobi-v1` method/version, caller-supplied positive finite absolute and
  relative residual limits, and a positive inner iteration limit. There is no
  hidden production tolerance or convergence default.
- The inner solve uses preallocated arrays and synchronous Jacobi updates over
  the frozen P4-T02 matrix-free operator. It accepts a finite nonnegative
  solution only when the global infinity absolute residual or relative
  residual meets the caller policy; exhaustion fails closed.
- The outer step computes current production from the supplied immutable state,
  solves group 1 before group 2, computes trial production, updates
  `k_next = k_current * trial_production / current_production`, computes trial
  fission power, and scales both trial flux vectors to the target watts.
  Production and power are recomputed from the normalized vectors before the
  new immutable state is returned; stale pre-normalization values are not
  published.
- Omitted initial flux vectors use deterministic all-ones vectors. Supplied
  vectors must be paired, dimensionally exact, finite, componentwise
  nonnegative, and produce finite positive fission power. Returned flux
  collections are defensive read-only copies.
- Invalid state ownership, invalid values, zero production/power, nonfinite
  arithmetic, inner nonconvergence, and `Int32.MaxValue` iteration-counter
  overflow return deterministic diagnostics without throwing from the
  contract boundary.
- The P4-T03 scope stops before outer convergence diagnostics, reference/golden
  comparisons, refuelling, depletion, kinetics, RRS, Unity behavior, and
  performance optimization.
