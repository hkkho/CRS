## Assumptions and design choices

- `BurnupCoefficientTableV1` v1 is reused as the runtime-facing projection;
  the candidate does not define a second runtime table or public pack schema.
- Material coefficient rows carry the existing SI units: cross sections and
  downscatter `m^-1`, burnup `J/kg_HM`, `ChiGroup1` dimensionless, and energy
  per fission `J`. Node volumes `m^3` and conductances `m^2` remain separate.
- Exact-knot/linear interpolation and fail-closed out-of-range behavior remain
  the existing Core implementation; R3 introduces no alternate interpolator,
  extrapolation, clamp, smoothing, repair, or hidden default.
- External Candu6.x2m output may be offline candidate/calibration evidence under
  the confirmed redistribution rights, but direct source-field admission into
  P2-T02 is retired by R2D.
- R4 may build a deterministic candidate converter/pack only when every source
  field, unit, transform, identity, and provenance is explicit. Missing
  topology, volume, conductance, energy, or normalization authority remains a
  stop condition rather than an inferred value.
