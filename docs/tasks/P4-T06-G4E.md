## Assumptions and design choices

- The committed G4D artifact and manifest are the sole consumer inputs. The
  project-file entries copy them and the frozen source/pack/benchmark inputs
  into test output; the tests do not discover private host paths.
- Exact hashes are asserted for the copied inputs and for both G4D outputs.
  The manifest must bind the artifact hash and the same source/pack/benchmark
  identities.
- The consumer accepts only the three G4D `CandidateSnapshot` cases and 57
  `Deferred` records. The refuelled, RRS, and poison cases remain outside this
  artifact boundary exactly as recorded by G4A/G4C.
- P2-T05 typed scopes, units, payload schemas, state bindings, and canonical
  order-key fields are consumed as identity/schema evidence. The tests check
  finite normalization-related fields but introduce no acceptance inequality
  or replacement golden value.
- No P1-T08 physics row is used as an executable oracle by this test-only task.
