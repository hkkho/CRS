## Assumptions and design choices

- The approved P8-T03 authority is synthetic gameplay data only. Its point
  weights are not physical energy, stability, fuelling, or plant-performance
  quantities.
- The explicit state-segment integral is evaluated over the P8-T02 simulation
  horizon. Scripted events split a segment at their exact simulation time;
  player actions take effect at the P8-T02 control-tick commit boundary.
- A turn's event-capacity count is the sum of committed actions, scripted
  events, and loss records. Exceeding the approved capacity returns an invalid
  result rather than silently truncating evidence.
- Score accumulation is performed per deterministic control tick, while the
  wrapper accepts arbitrary caller wall-duration chunks. This preserves score,
  outcome, and simulation-step equivalence for 100 ms versus 50+50 ms calls.
- Cause prioritizes loss, then scripted event, then player action, otherwise
  elapsed play. Effects mention only approved proxy deltas, score movement, or
  scenario outcome; they never claim a physical plant response.
- The installed direct .NET SDK 10.0.303/MSBuild and VSTest host were used as
  a local fallback because repository `global.json` requests 10.0.302, which
  is unavailable on this machine. This does not change repository behavior.
