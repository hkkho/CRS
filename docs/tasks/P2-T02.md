## Assumptions and design choices

- P2-T02 is a Sol-led mathematical specification task. A Luna implementation
  worker was not substituted because the task fixes equations, signs, units,
  indexing/order, normalization, and convergence contracts before runtime code.
- The candidate binds all unknowns and coefficients to the P2-T01 topology and
  uses canonical node, group, local-face, boundary-face, edge, and scalar
  reduction order. It does not infer topology or solver inputs from P1 compact
  exports.
- Fission absorption is included in `Sigma_a`; `Sigma_f` is its subset and
  `nuSigma_f` is the same reaction weighted by yield. V1 rejects upscatter,
  non-finite or negative data, mismatched fission fields, failed inner solves,
  nonconvergence, and every attempted clamp.
- Power normalization is separate from `k`. The initial flux shape is
  normalized before iteration zero, and every later normalized state recomputes
  fission production and integrated production before the next source solve.
- Inner and outer residuals use zero-safe global infinity scaling. The caller
  supplies tolerances; this task does not select production defaults or tune
  against DRAGON5/DONJON5 output.
- All worked values are explicitly synthetic placeholders and are not copied
  from private/raw listings, DRAGON5, DONJON5, or golden data.

### Independent Sol review and review fixes

The same fresh read-only max-reasoning `gpt-5.6-sol` reviewer performed all
dispositions. The initial review returned `FAIL` with seven findings:

1. High: normalized `F` and `Q` state was not unambiguously reused.
2. High: absorption/fission definitions and invariants were incomplete.
3. High: inner linear-solve acceptance and failure limits were underdefined.
4. High: conductance keys and boundary behavior were not fully bound.
5. Medium: deterministic local and reduction ordering was incomplete.
6. Medium: the source-shape convergence metric was not independent of `k`.
7. Medium: worked examples lacked complete numeric continuation, conservation,
   and permutation checks.

The candidate was corrected and the same reviewer returned a second `FAIL` with
five residual findings: scalar inner residual norms/units, initial-normalization
agreement, `Sigma_f`/`nuSigma_f` zero consistency, stronger permutation and
numeric nonconvergence examples, and explicit no-clamp semantics. Those were
corrected. The next same-reviewer pass returned `FAIL` with one High zero-source
componentwise-relative-residual defect and one Low flux-positivity wording
defect. The candidate was corrected to use global zero-safe infinity scaling for
inner and outer residuals, absolute-or-relative inner acceptance, and finite
componentwise-nonnegative flux terminology with strictly positive total
production/power. The final same-reviewer disposition was `PASS` with no
High/Medium/Low findings; the reviewer confirmed final candidate SHA
`9822aaed0f801049406563f76b1f4a5b67b40d09c601a12e87c8e4b54593d6b2` and no
files written during review.
