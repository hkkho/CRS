# P5-T12 - source-digest authentication and strict refuelling boundary

## Outcome

Status: INCOMPLETE pending the same-context high-effort G5 rerun.

P5-T12 addresses the two High findings returned by the corrected G5 review.
Complete accepted power snapshots now carry and serialize a canonical
snapshot-stable complete-inventory digest plus a canonical burnup/energy
digest. Snapshot acceptance and the complete burnup transition both recompute
and verify those digests, so a reconstructed inventory cannot retain accepted
power/history bindings while changing energy, initial burnup, mass, identity,
location, or complete nuclide state. The digest checks occur before any
proposed burnup replacement or lifecycle commit.

The public no-table complete-refuelling overload now fails closed after source
preconditions and before constructing a successful result. Every successful
complete-state refuelling commit therefore goes through the table-supplied
strict boundary and performs/stores post-shift coefficient lookups. Existing
valid compatibility coverage was migrated to that strict boundary, while
stale-source tests continue to verify the earlier source-digest diagnostics.

Effectiveness: SUCCESS for the implementation and validation slice; final gate
closure remains pending the required independent review.

## Assumptions and design choices

- The snapshot-stable inventory digest intentionally excludes the ephemeral
  current power/history/coefficient binding that is appended by accepting the
  snapshot; it includes persistent identity/location, material, initial and
  cumulative energy, mass, insertion time, state version, and complete
  nuclide-envelope bytes. The separate burnup/energy digest explicitly covers
  the energy projection used by positive-duration advancement.
- The existing full inventory digest used by prepared refuelling requests is
  preserved, so source changes to accepted power/history still invalidate a
  prepared request. The new strict refuelling path additionally requires a
  version-matched coefficient table for every live material.
- No equation, unit, sign, convergence rule, tolerance, golden value, or
  external authority was selected or changed. The frozen left-endpoint rule
  and existing ReducedModel boundary remain the authorities.
- Applicable P1-T08 literature rows remain `S1-R07`, `S4-R03`, `S4-R04`,
  `S5-R05`, and `S6-R05`; they are context/methodology or candidate-design
  evidence only.
