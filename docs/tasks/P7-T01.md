## Assumptions and design choices

- The owner authorization selected `P7-T01` as the smallest bounded Phase 7A
  task: approved point-kinetics/quasi-static amplitude update only.
- `P2-T04` is the runtime authority. Delayed-neutron coefficients, data
  identity/version/digest, initial values, reference power, reactivity, and
  spatial binding are explicit caller inputs; no nuclear constants or hidden
  initial conditions are supplied by this task.
- The transition applies the approved finite-double left-endpoint Euler
  equations. It rejects invalid intervals, stale data, nonrepresentable time,
  step-index overflow, non-finite or signed-negative-zero intermediate/audit
  terms, negative results, and non-finite/nonnegative state results. It does
  not clamp or silently change the caller's timestep.
- A state may exist before a spatial solve is bound, but a positive-duration
  transition requires the complete exact solve identity, state version, and
  feedback-overlay binding required by P2-T04.
- Applicable P1-T08 digest rows are `S1-R09`, `S4-R05`, `S4-R06`, and `S5-R08`.
  They provide methodology, context, candidate case-design, or blocked
  comparison evidence only; none is an approved equation, coefficient,
  normalization, initial-condition, tolerance, or golden-data authority for
  this task. The approved P2-T04 specification remains controlling.
