## Assumptions and design choices

- The case is representative reduced-model evidence, not an external CANDU
  numeric baseline. The source pack remains synthetic candidate evidence.
- The Core topology and solver contracts are consumed exactly as frozen; no
  equation, sign, unit, public schema, or runtime dependency was changed.
- The two S4 histories are represented as post-event state plus explicit
  inserted/moved/discharged bundle audits. I/Xe and kinetics are marked
  `NotCovered`, not approximated.
- RRS and poison are prescribed absorption overlays using explicit P2-T04-style
  map entries. No controller, liquid-zone state machine, adjuster sequence,
  thermal-hydraulic behavior, or safety behavior was added.
- The first 512-iteration candidate run failed closed on the fresh scenario
  with residual `2.9425867761608646e-6` and source-shape change
  `1.889061143033055e-7`. The definition was corrected by increasing the
  caller-owned maximum outer iteration budget to 4096; no residual, comparison,
  or golden threshold was loosened. The final fresh scenario converged in 1823
  iterations.
