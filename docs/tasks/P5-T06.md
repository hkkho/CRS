## Assumptions and design choices

- The approved P2-T02 coefficient/operator and P2-T03 burnup/table boundaries
  remain authoritative. P2-T04 supplies the exact cadence and lifecycle event
  ordering constraints; no new equation, tolerance, scheduler behavior, or
  golden value was selected.
- The bounded transition treats every live node in the supplied stencil as the
  affected set. The caller supplies validated tables, explicit positive node
  volumes, conductances, target power, initial state, and solver policies; the
  contract invents no defaults.
- The next cadence event is calculated from the stored epoch, positive period,
  and integer index using the stored binary64 values. Nonfinite,
  unrepresentable, overflowing, or non-progressing advancement fails closed.
- The existing conductance source is copied in canonical stencil order. The
  transition does not derive geometry coefficients or replace leakage when a
  burnup table lookup changes node material coefficients.
- Exact `CoreTopology` instance identity is required between the lifecycle and
  conductance stencil in addition to dimension checks. This prevents a
  same-sized foreign topology from being accepted under the lifecycle digest.
- `Digest32` and table checksums remain opaque loader/data-pack identities. A
  canonical byte serializer or checksum algorithm was not invented here.
- The applicable P1-T08 rows are `S1-R04`, `S1-R05`, `S5-R03`, `S5-R04`, and
  `S5-R05`. They are background/methodology or candidate-design evidence only;
  no literature number, coefficient, tolerance, or golden value was admitted.
- `G2` remains exactly `FORCED CLOSED / WAIVED`. P5-T06 is eligible under the
  non-critical gate-overlap policy and is independent of the blocked P4-T06
  DONJON5 licensing and solver-mapping issue.
