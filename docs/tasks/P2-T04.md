## Assumptions and design choices

- P2-T04 is Sol-led because it fixes equations, signs, units, state ownership,
  cadence, influence-map targets, and feedback coupling. No Luna implementation
  worker was substituted.
- V1 uses a dimensionless amplitude `a` multiplying the P2-T02 shape normalized
  to caller-supplied `P_ref`; actual bundle/node powers are `a*P_shape`, and
  those actual values are the only values accepted by P2-T03 power snapshots,
  histories, and burnup integration.
- V1 point kinetics and I/Xe use explicit left-endpoint Euler with a caller-
  supplied, versioned stability policy. The exact `H` minimum and deterministic
  ceil/remainder subdivision are specified; no production timestep, tolerance,
  nuclear constant, or automatic hidden repair is selected.
- I/Xe atom inventories are authoritative and number densities are derived from
  the exact P2-T02 node volume. Material/table-keyed nuclide data and the
  xenon-free base-table marker prevent unit ambiguity and dynamic Xe double
  counting.
- RRS and device maps are local, direct non-fission absorption overlays applied
  before P2-T02. The resulting effective `k` is the sole reactivity input to
  kinetics. Reference-state digests and single-owner map rules prevent duplicate
  device/feedback effects. Regional observables supply total-power and tilt
  control; explicit actuator command/state delay, rate, saturation, and sign
  contracts are data-driven.
- Temperature, purity, liquid-zone, adjuster, and bulk-poison branches remain
  explicit even when disabled. Disabled overlays contribute exactly zero;
  dynamic thermal/chemistry behavior and safety-system behavior remain deferred.
- All numeric examples and map/device values are explicitly synthetic and are
  not copied from DRAGON5, DONJON5, private listings, or golden data.

### Independent Sol review and review fixes

A fresh empty-context max-reasoning `gpt-5.6-sol` reviewer performed one initial
review and the required same-reviewer final dispositions after correction passes.
The initial review returned `FAIL` with ten findings:

1. Critical: scalar-only maps could not produce spatial control or tilt.
2. High: actual amplitude power was not fully bound to P2-T03 snapshots/burnup
   boundaries and rollback semantics were ambiguous.
3. High: explicit Euler lacked an admissibility/stability timestep policy.
4. High: the base coefficient xenon basis was not enforceable.
5. High: direct feedback ownership permitted double counting.
6. High: RRS actuator command/state, delay, rate, and polarity contracts were
   incomplete.
7. Medium: device/branch schema cardinality and fields were incomplete.
8. Medium: deterministic event/map/cadence ordering was underdefined.
9. Medium: the synthetic Xe dimensional check was wrong.
10. Medium: nuclide density volume and material/data binding were ambiguous.

One consolidated correction pass resolved these. The first same-reviewer final
disposition found four residuals: the effective absorption equation was repeated
in contradictory forms, the bulk-poison target index was shadowed, substep
partitioning was not uniquely constructed, and actuator/zone/poison/purity/tilt
edge semantics had narrow gaps. A second consolidated correction pass made the
effective equation authoritative, fixed the index, defined exact `H` and
ceil/remainder construction, completed command queues and rate modes, bounded
purity, and consumed `K_TI`. The same reviewer then returned final `PASS` with
no High/Medium/Low findings and confirmed candidate SHA
`75fdfc783b881933d8ec0bb12c5855818a205caec203e0d79d70e138d1eadc3c`. No review
agent wrote files.
