## Cases and numerical differences

### Case A - S4 lattice k-effective cross-check

- Printed DRAGON: `1.72972`; printed SERPENT: `1.73509` with sigma `0.00042`.
- Synthetic minimax midpoint: `1.732405`.
- Differences: `+2.685 mk` (`0.1552%`) to DRAGON and `-2.685 mk`
  (`0.1547%`) to SERPENT.
- The midpoint minimizes maximum absolute distance to the printed pair; it is
  not a reproduced solver result or a tolerance.

### Case B - S5 300-day lattice depletion handoff

- Reported input: `31.9713 kW/kg` for `300 days`; reported rounded exit:
  `9.6 GWd/t`.
- Independent energy identity output: `9.59139 GWd/t`.
- Difference: `0.00861 GWd/t` absolute and `0.0897%` relative.
- The output contains every day from 0 through 300 and retains six reported
  initial k-difference values as target evidence. No tolerance is inferred.

### Case C - S1 full-core schedule and alternate-cladding summary

- Reported schedule: 380 channels, 12 bundles/channel, alternating coolant
  directions, 8-bundle shift, 4 channels/day, 300 full-power days.
- Synthetic schedule: 1200 channel events and 9600 shifted bundles, under the
  explicit convention of one eight-bundle shift per refuelling event.
- Reported summary replays: `+3.4 mk` reactivity change and `-246 kW` channel
  power change; `+1.25 ppm` is the midpoint of the reported `+1.1` to `+1.4`
  ppm boron interval.

### Case D - S4 full-core refuelling aggregate fixture

- Seven Holmes Table 5.2 simulation aggregates are replayed exactly as
  project-authored fixture values beside the time-average reference.
- The deterministic schedule contains 408 events in 100 days, exactly `4.08`
  channels/day, with eight bundles per event.
- Differences to the time-average reference are preserved as source comparison
  values, not solver error tolerances.

  kWh/kg-per-GWd/t conversion values; the rounded source value is not forced.

  channel maps or source solver trajectories.

  schedule totals, and midpoint/unit invariants only.
