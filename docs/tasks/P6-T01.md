## Assumptions and design choices

- The frozen P2-T04/P2-T05 contract is the authority: logical IDs are exactly
  `[0,13]`, physical assembly IDs are exactly `[0,5]`, all six physical IDs must
  be represented, and no mapping is inferred from array order.
- Grouping and zone/system digests are SHA-256 over self-excluding canonical
  bytes. A supplied grouping digest is required and checked; no production map
  values or golden numbers are selected.
- The queue binding stores queue identity, the `CANDU-ZONE-QUEUE-V1` schema
  identity, the complete queue-state digest, and the approved P2-T05 typed
  `Entity.Branch` owner-key shape. Complete queue contents, projections, event
  allocation, motion, consume, and rollback transitions remain P6-T06 work.
- `AvailableCommandFillFraction` and `LastMotionTime` are not duplicated in
  `LiquidZoneStateV1`; they remain queue-owned projections as required by
  P2-T04/P2-T05.
- `LiquidZoneDisabledZeroAssertionV1` is intentionally a zone-level assertion.
  It exposes exact `0.0` overlay evidence without inventing map weights. The
  final branch-level P2-T05 `DisabledZeroV1` observable adapter remains later
  work.
- Synthetic test identities/digests are contract fixtures only. They are not
  runtime physics data, reference values, or golden evidence.
