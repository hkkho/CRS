## Assumptions and design choices

- The scenario package is derived evidence, not a new physics or data
  authority. It binds the four approved synthetic input packages and declares
  `VALIDATED_P6-T06`, `VALIDATED_P6-T07_TEST_FIXTURES`, and `RESERVED_G6`
  boundaries explicitly.
- The centered perturbation uses the approved 800 W synthetic measurement,
  produces exact controller commands `0.72` total and `0.50` tilt, and records
  pre-delay state `0.50` followed by post-delay state `0.60` under the approved
  delay/rate contract.
- The regional-tilt fixture uses the approved controller coefficients without
  inventing new gains. For the 1000 W / 0.60 / 0.40 measurement, the frozen
  coefficients produce `0.50` total and `0.61` tilt. The test records distinct
  signed left/right local overlay values and byte-for-byte replay equality.
- Zone saturation exercises the shared P6-T06 queue transition with a
  requested fraction above the explicit upper bound, then verifies the
  bounded final fill fraction and `UpperBound` diagnostic. It does not add a
  queue allocation or transition rule.
- Adjuster motion begins from the approved `0.50` state and uses separate
  explicit post-motion states at `1.0` and `0.0` for local overlay evaluation;
  this keeps queue-owned delayed motion and map-owned overlay state distinct.
  The scenario package's queue/owner IDs and post-delay duration are asserted
  by the evidence test.
- Poison recovery uses separate approved add and withdraw actions with exact
  mass accounting: 0.5 kg to 1.0 kg during add, then to 0.9 kg during the
  ten-second recovery interval, giving 0.18 kg/m^3 in the 5.0 m^3 moderator
  volume. No decay, transport, cleanup law, or hidden chemistry is introduced.
- The refuelling check verifies only the frozen same-time event rank order
  `Burnup`, `Refuelling`, `ControllerCommandGeneration`, `ActuatorMotion`,
  `BranchUpdate`; it does not execute or mutate refuelling state.
- Applicable P1-T08 literature rows are `S1-R08`, `S4-R05`, `S6-R02`, `S6-R05`,
  and `S6-R08`. They provide device-role, regulating-system, adjuster,
  candidate-design, or limitation context only; none is a runtime equation,
  map, controller, comparison, or golden-data authority for this synthetic
  task.
