# P7-T03 - Phase 7A integration stability policy and timestep limits

## Outcome

Status: COMPLETE

P7-T03 defines the caller-supplied, engine-neutral explicit-Euler stability
policy required by P2-T04. The policy validates canonical finite positive
inputs, computes the authoritative five-term minimum `H`, checks direct and
dimensionless substep bounds plus active decay/prompt-rate coverage, and
constructs the exact deterministic `ceil(Delta_t_gap / H)` schedule. The
schedule has `H` for its first `n-1` entries and the prescribed scalar-order
remainder for its final entry. No timestep default, tolerance, nuclear

## Assumptions and design choices

- `docs/spec/kinetics-xenon-rrs-feedback-v1.md` is the runtime authority. The
  policy's seven fields and identity are caller inputs; no P2-T04 default is
  invented.
- `H` is computed in the approved order from
  `MaximumKineticStep`, `MaximumNuclideStep`, `MaximumRRSStep`,
  `MaximumDimensionlessStep / MaximumDecayRate`, and
  `MaximumDimensionlessStep / MaximumPromptRate`. A policy is rejected when a
  required term is noncanonical, nonfinite, or nonpositive.
- Actual maximum decay and prompt rates are explicit method inputs. Values
  above the declared policy envelopes fail closed; the implementation never
  infers missing coverage.
- `IntegrationSubstepScheduleV1` is a logical immutable `IReadOnlyList<double>`
  rather than a mutable allocation. Its `Int32` count is an explicit
  representability boundary; a count outside that representation fails closed.
  It stores the final remainder computed as
  `Delta_t_gap - (n - 1) * H`, with no repair or alternate partition.
- Existing P7-T01/P7-T02 Euler transition equations remain the approved
  transition authorities. This task supplies their common policy validator and
  scheduler; scenario/history orchestration and controlled xenon coupling stay
  in their named Phase 7 tasks.
- Applicable P1-T08 literature rows: `S1-R09` (135Xe methodology/context only),
  `S4-R05` (refuelling/reactivity/device context only), `S4-R06` (blocked
  candidate comparison evidence with no approved baseline), and `S5-R08`
  (candidate case-design context with no runtime mapping). None authorizes a
  timestep, rate, tolerance, or runtime equation; P2-T04 remains authoritative.
