## Assumptions and design choices

- The required literature rows are `S1-R09`, `S4-R05`, `S4-R06`, and `S5-R08`.
  They were used as search anchors and applicability context, not as executable
  oracles or golden values.
- The external case route is `NOT ADMITTED / DEFERRED` because the missing
  exact-case, nuclear-data, mapping, output, and rights fields are critical to
  authority admission. The published CNS material is contextual; the NEA
  benchmark comparators are non-CANDU reactor classes or separately accessed
  packages.
- The synthetic case uses only existing approved Phase 7 contracts and
  fixture-scale conventions. Its values are not claimed to represent CANDU
  plant physics, a published result, or a production tolerance.
- The standalone generator deliberately has no `ReactorSim.Core` project
  reference. It independently evaluates the frozen left-endpoint Euler
  contracts, writes canonical JSON, and validates definition/artifact hashes.
- Candidate and approved statuses are separate. Approval means only
  Synthetic/test-only regression consumption after G7A re-entry; it does not
  replace the external authority gap.
- No equation, unit, sign convention, convergence rule, tolerance, runtime
  state, public schema, safety behavior, or Phase 7B behavior was changed.
