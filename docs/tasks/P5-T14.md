# P5-T14 - lifecycle-authenticated complete snapshot digest correction

## Outcome

Status: INCOMPLETE pending the required same-context high-effort G5 rerun.

P5-T14 closes the High finding from the fourth bounded G5 review. Complete
snapshot acceptance now stores the pre-accept inventory digest, full post-accept
inventory digest, and burnup/energy digest directly on the accepted lifecycle.
Those lifecycle fields are included in the canonical lifecycle state digest.
The positive-duration burnup transition first verifies that the supplied
snapshot carries exactly the three digest values authenticated by the current
lifecycle, then verifies the live inventory against the lifecycle's stored
post-accept digest. A reconstructed inventory and matching caller-created
snapshot can no longer bypass the accepted lifecycle by reusing its IDs and
unchanged solver snapshot digest.

The new focused regression removes an earlier accepted history record, creates
a matching forged snapshot carrying the truncated inventory digest, and
verifies that burnup rejects the pair at the lifecycle-binding boundary with
the source lifecycle unchanged. The prior full-history mutation regression and
all Phase 5/T3/G4-R6 evidence remain green.

Effectiveness: SUCCESS for the implementation and validation slice; final G5
closure remains pending the independent same-context review.

## Assumptions and design choices

- The lifecycle is the trusted accepted-snapshot boundary already used for
  snapshot IDs, versions, state, coefficient, topology, data-pack, and solver
  snapshot identities. The new digest fields follow that same immutable
  acceptance path and are invalidated by later core/burnup/refuelling commits.
- `InventoryDigest` remains the full pre-accept inventory digest;
  `AcceptedInventoryDigest` remains the full post-accept digest; and
  `BurnupEnergyDigest` remains the energy projection. All three are stored
  together so a partial caller-controlled replacement cannot be accepted.
- The frozen left-endpoint rule remains `Delta_E = P * Delta_t`, with
  `B = InitialBurnup + CumulativeFissionEnergy / HeavyMetalMass`; no numerical
  authority or tolerance was selected.
- Applicable P1-T08 literature rows are `S1-R07`, `S4-R03`, `S4-R04`, `S5-R05`,
  and `S6-R05`; they remain context/methodology or candidate-design evidence,
  not runtime or golden authority.
- Direct CANDU/DONJON5 production authority, full-core coverage, mobile, and
  Unity evidence remain outside this bounded ReducedModel/Core task.
