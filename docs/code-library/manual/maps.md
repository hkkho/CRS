# Read the core

Use + and − to zoom, scroll to pan, and Fit core to return to the full map. Hover or keyboard-focus a channel for current power, peak bundle power, ripple and estimated average exit burnup. Escape dismisses the tooltip. Exit burnup averages the eight outgoing positions from the shared movement plan at current burnup; it is not a next-day forecast. Channel readings also remain available in the inspector.

## Burnup

Five contrasting bands: below 2, 2–4, 4–6, 6–8, and at least 8 MWd/kg HM, from dark to white. The shortlist ranks average channel burnup.

## Channel kW / Bundle kW

Absolute channel power or the hottest bundle in each channel. Discrete dark, bronze, orange, red and bright-red bands. Red starts at 90% of the published limit; bright red shakes at 97%. These are display warnings, not safety predictions. Reduced motion keeps a static dashed warning. The legend states each threshold.

## Ripple %

Channel power as a percentage of its fixed reference. Orange spans 95–105%; darker bands are below reference, red is above 105%, and bright red starts at 115%. This reference varies by channel.

## Channel profiles

Power and burnup run from position 01 at End A to position 12 at End B. Open Poison & device detail for iodine, xenon and absorber overlap. Power plots retain a labelled limit even when it falls outside the plotted range.

## Devices

Toggle amber adjusters on the face. Switch End A / End B LZC planes to see cyan tube compartments and water levels. Geometry comes from the shared simulation.

## History

The view dropdown shows power peaks/output, discharged burnup, zone levels, tilt, reactivity, poison, fuel and score. Toggle traces and choose a time window. Move View time or select a graph sample to inspect a recorded state. Return to live before changing the plan. Up to 4,096 observations are retained in this browser session; a new shift clears them.
