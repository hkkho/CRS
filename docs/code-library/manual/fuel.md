# Choose and queue fuel

A fuel order inserts eight fresh bundles with the channel's flow and discharges eight at the opposite end. Four bundles stay in the channel and carry their burnup and poison history. Adjacent channels have opposite flow directions. Time-based free practice has unlimited fresh stock and still counts all bundles used.

Select a channel and choose **Queue fuel**, double-click it, or press **Enter** or **R** on the map. The next unsolved step takes the waiting orders as a batch; the waiting queue holds up to eight channels. Yellow insets mark waiting orders, blue insets mark submitted orders, and a white outline marks the selected channel. Repeat the action or choose **Remove order** to remove a waiting order; submitted orders cannot be removed. The fuel receipt reports delivered bundles and batch points when the result appears.

## Choosing fuel

- Look for high burnup and enough headroom in the paired liquid zones. Each discharged bundle scores according to its own burnup, so a channel average is a guide.
- Check nearby power. Fresh fuel can increase power in the channel and its neighbours.
- Spread orders when the core is near a limit. All orders in a batch are applied together. If the combined result crosses a limit, the run ends.

The Maps and readings chapter explains the available measurements. The Limits and score chapter explains the consequences of each move.
